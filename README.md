# Obsidian Patches

Small fixes for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

Obsidian Patches removes the black bars on wide screens, skips the intro, the login screen and the planet scans, speeds up the mission end, pauses the game in solo missions, and fixes the recoil and sprint animations.

## How to use

Obsidian Patches comes with Obsidian, in the Obsidian Kit, switched on. Each fix is an option in Obsidian and takes effect at the next launch. Four of them can also be changed in game, under **Mods** in the pause menu, and apply at once.
